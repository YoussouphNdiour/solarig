Solar pumping

A borehole, a basin or a field to irrigate: we size, supply and install the complete solar pumping system, from the panels to the wellhead, with no diesel and no electricity bill.

What we do

Our services

  • Study and flow test

    Water need, dynamic level and head survey: we size the pump and the solar array precisely.

  • Submersible solar pumps

    Hybrid AC/DC from 1,500 W to 22 kW, 3,000 W DC pumps, 7.5 and 11 kW borehole pumps: they run on sun or mains depending on the model.

  • Panels, structures and controller

    Ground or rack-mounted solar array, MPPT controller, protections, submersible cables and threaded PVC column pipes.

  • Installation and commissioning

    Structure installation, panel mounting, pump lowering, wiring and flow test at the borehole outlet.

  • Follow-up and repairs

    Inspections, fault diagnosis, pump or controller replacement, spare parts available.

Who is it for?

  • Market gardeners and farms
  • Livestock farmers and watering
  • Villages, schools and places of worship
  • Pool and pond owners
  • Drilling and water engineering companies

    Visit and flow test
